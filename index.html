Questions? We have answers.

Milo kyu use karein?

Yahan aap profile ke saath hobbies chunte ho, aur Milo wahi log dikhata hai jinke interests aapse milte hain. Isse pehli baat-cheet ke liye topic mil jata hai.

Isse kya fayda hoga?

Same hobbies wale log upar dikhte hain, same city wale pehle. Friends aur Dating alag modes hain, to aap sirf wahi dekhte ho jo chahiye.

Kya koi problem ya risk aa sakta hai?

Kisi bhi online site par fake profile ya scam ka risk hota hai. Kabhi OTP, password ya paise kisi ko mat do, aur pehli baar public jagah par milo. Block/report feature har chat mein hai, aur admin reporting system se suspicious accounts ban kiye jate hain.

Kya Milo free hai?

Haan, abhi signup, profile aur suggestions free hain. Milo mein paid plans nahi hain. Aage chalke site ka kharcha ads se chalega.

Mera data aur password safe hai?

Password hum nahi dekh sakte, wo Supabase mein encrypted rehta hai. Aapki date of birth aur email dusre users ko nahi dikhte; sirf naam, username, city aur hobbies dikhte hain.

Kaun join kar sakta hai?

Sirf 18+ log. Age signup ke time database mein check hoti hai.

Kya yahan chat hai?

Haan. Kisi ko request bhejo, accept hone par chat khulti hai. Block aur report har chat mein hai. Calls aur paid plans Milo mein nahi honge.
